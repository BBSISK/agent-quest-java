# Agent Quest rule book

## Skill checks
Roll a d20 and add the relevant modifier (strength for feats of strength).
15 or higher succeeds. A natural 20 always succeeds; a natural 1 always fails.

## Combat
Attacker rolls a d20 + strength. 12 or higher hits.
A rusty sword deals 1d6 damage; unarmed deals 1d3. Enemies act after the player each round.
Goblins have 7 HP and deal 1d4 damage on a hit.

## Magic
Each spell uses one spell slot. With no slots left, the spell fails.
Fireball: 3d6 fire damage to every enemy in a small area.
Healing word: restore 1d8 HP.
A full night's rest at an inn restores all spell slots and HP.

## Tavern
Ale costs 1 gold. A room for the night costs 5 gold.
Arm wrestling: both sides roll d20 + strength; higher total wins. A friendly wager is up to 5 gold.
